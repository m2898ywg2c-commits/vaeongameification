# Vaeon, in five minutes

Everything you need to know to use it. Nothing you do not.

---

## What Vaeon is for

Most training apps hand you a workout and never tell you whether it worked.

Vaeon does three things instead. It works out how you like to be coached, it builds your
plan from numbers you actually lifted rather than numbers off the internet, and at the end
of every block it tells you honestly whether you got anywhere.

It scores you on turning up, not on how much you lifted. Two sessions you actually did beat
five you meant to.

---

## Getting started

**1. Sign up.** Pick a screen name. That is what everyone else sees, never your real name.

**2. Choose your goals.** Up to two. If you already have a personal trainer, pick **Gym
ready** and Vaeon will count your sessions without telling you what to do.

**3. Say how many sessions a week.** Be honest. This is your pledge, and it is what you get
scored against. Picking five when you do three does not make you fitter, it just makes your
score worse.

**4. Take the assessment.** Three minutes, twelve statements. It gives you one of eight
training personalities, and that shapes your plan, your coaching voice and your colour.

**5. Put it on your home screen.** The dashboard offers this. It opens full screen with no
browser bar, and on an iPhone it is the only way notifications will ever reach you.

---

## Your first week is a testing week

**This is the important bit, and it is easy to get wrong.**

Week one has no prescribed weights on the main lifts. You find your own and log them. Every
week after that is a percentage of what you log now.

So do not max out. **Work up to a set of 8 to 10 reps you could have stopped two short of.**
If you grind out a heavy triple instead, the maths reads that as a much lower ceiling than
it should, and the whole block comes out too light.

Log what you actually did, not what you meant to do.

## Using it day to day

Open the app and the top of the dashboard tells you two things: anything you still need to
set up, then today's workout. Tap it.

**Logging a set.** Your target is already filled in. If you did something different, type
over it. Under each set you will see what you did last time, so you always know the number
to beat.

**The rest timer.** Pinned to the bottom of the session screen. Tap it between sets. It
keeps running if you lock your phone, switch apps or wander off, and it turns your colour
for the last ten seconds so you know to get back under the bar.

**Finishing.** Tick off every exercise and it logs the session for you. Or tap Finish.

**No gym today?** There is a "Train without a gym" option. Desk, hotel room, park. It counts.

---

## Streaks, and the grace week

Your streak counts weeks where you hit the number of sessions you pledged.

You get **one grace week per block**. Miss a week and it is spent automatically, and your
streak survives. Illness, travel and bad weeks are not failures and the app does not treat
them as such.

The dashboard tells you whether you still have yours.

---

## The social bits

**The leaderboard** scores everyone on the percentage of their own pledge they have hit, so
somebody doing two a week is compared fairly with somebody doing six. You can hide yourself
from it at any time and still see everyone else and send kudos.

**Kudos** is a tap on somebody's row. Emoji and a short line from a fixed list.

**The challenge** is a shared target for the whole group. It shows how many sessions we are
collectively short and how many of them were yours.

---

## Reminders

Settings, Reminders. One nudge a day at most, and none at all in a week where you have
already hit your sessions.

The time is set from the assessment question about when your body is at its best, and you
can change it to whatever actually fits.

On an iPhone, notifications only work if you added Vaeon to your home screen first. Without
that you still get the nudge on your dashboard when you open the app.

---

## Light and dark

Vaeon is dark by default. **Tap the sun in the top left of any screen to switch to light**,
and the moon to switch back.

If light is easier for you to read, use it. That is not a lesser version of the app.

---

## The end of a block

Six weeks, or eight if you are Gym ready. When it finishes, the dashboard turns green and
offers your block summary: sessions done against sessions pledged, personal bests, which
lifts climbed, which stalled, and what your bodyweight did.

Then it rolls you into the next block with your new numbers as the baseline.

---

## Things worth knowing

**Your data is yours.** Settings, Your data, Download my data. Everything Vaeon holds about
you in one file, whenever you want it.

**Change your mind whenever.** Goals, sessions per week, training days, block start date are
all editable in Settings. Retake the assessment as often as you like.

**Nothing here is medical advice.** If something hurts, stop, and talk to somebody
qualified. The full disclaimer is in Settings.
