# Vaeon type characters, creative brief

For Artlist, Midjourney, Nano Banana or anything else. The prompts are the small part. The
locked rules below are what make eight images look like one family instead of eight
unrelated pictures.

---

## Step one: the pilot

**Generate the Hunter twice, animal and human, and compare.** Do not generate eight of
anything until one is right.

The Hunter is the right pilot. It is the strongest colour in the set, it is the type James
holds so he will spot a wrong note fastest, and both readings are obvious: a falcon, or a
lone tracker.

### Prompt A, the falcon

> A peregrine falcon as a stylised 3D character mascot, perched alert and forward-leaning
> mid-hunt. Three-quarter view, head turned to camera, confident and focused rather than
> aggressive. Soft studio lighting from the upper left, gentle rim light on the right edge.
> Plumage in warm orange #FF8C42 with deep burnt umber #7A2E0E markings, no other hues.
> Clean vector-friendly forms, smooth surfaces, minimal texture detail, no feather noise.
> Full body, centred, generous margin, transparent background. Flat even lighting on the
> silhouette so the shape reads clearly at small sizes.

### Prompt B, the human

> A stylised 3D character mascot of a lone trail runner mid-stride, athletic but not
> idealised, three-quarter view, head turned to camera, confident and focused. Soft studio
> lighting from the upper left, gentle rim light on the right edge. Kit in warm orange
> #FF8C42 with deep burnt umber #7A2E0E panels, no other hues. Clean vector-friendly forms,
> smooth surfaces, minimal texture. Full body, centred, generous margin, transparent
> background.

Same lighting, same angle, same palette, same crop in both. That is the point. If the two
differ on anything except the subject, the comparison is not telling you what you think.

### What to judge them on

1. Squint at each one. Does the **silhouette alone** still read as that type?
2. Shrink to 40px. Which one survives?
3. Put them side by side with the Vaeon mark. Which one looks like it came from the same
   place?
4. Which one would you rather have on the leaderboard next to your sister's?

## The locked rules

Everything below stays identical across all eight, or they will not look like a set. Lock
these before generating number two.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Camera | Three-quarter view, eye level, head turned to camera |
| Lighting | Key from upper left, soft rim from the right, no hard shadow |
| Crop | Full body, centred, generous margin all round |
| Background | Transparent |
| Palette | Two colours per character, that type's pair, nothing else |
| Surface | Smooth, vector-friendly, minimal texture |
| Mood | Confident and capable. Never aggressive, never comic, never cute |
| Output | Square, at least 2048px, PNG with alpha |

The palette rule is the one that will make it feel designed rather than bought. Each
character wears its own type's colours and no others.

## After the images exist

**Redraw them as SVG.** This is not optional polish, it solves three problems at once.

- **Size.** The orb renders at eleven sizes from 24px to 140px. Raster will not survive that
  range; vector will.
- **Colour.** Vector lets each character carry its type colour natively and swap for the
  light theme, rather than being repainted.
- **Ownership.** Purely AI-generated images may attract no UK copyright, because there is no
  human author exercising creative control. Significant human modification restores copyright
  in the human-added parts. Redrawing is that modification, and it means you own the result.

Budget a few hundred pounds with an illustrator rather than a few thousand. Give them the
eight renders plus this brief.

## How they get used in the app

**The orb stays.** Do not replace it. A detailed character is a smudge at 24px and
unreadable at 38px on the leaderboard.

Two tiers:

- **Under about 60px**, the existing coloured orb. Dashboard header, leaderboard rows, the
  type card.
- **Above that**, the character. Assessment result, the type page, block end, the share card.

That also means this ships incrementally. One character is useful on its own; you do not
need all eight before anything works.

---

## Timing, honestly

Four of twelve users have still never logged a session. Eight beautiful animals will not
change that. This is identity work, identity is the sharable part, and it is worth doing
properly, but it belongs after the six-week test tells you whether the types predict
anything at all.
